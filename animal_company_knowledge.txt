ANIMAL COMPANY - FULL KNOWLEDGE BASE
Official Store: https://www.meta.com/experiences/animal-company/7190422614401072/

=== BASIC INFO ===
- Free multiplayer social VR survival game
- Developed by Wooster Games
- Released on Meta Quest (App Lab) on July 3, 2024
- Still in Early Access
- Also available on SteamVR for $20
- Extremely popular on Quest (often top earning game)
- Players control customizable thick-cheeked animals
- Inspired by Gorilla Tag (movement) + Lethal Company (gameplay loop)

=== MAIN MODES ===
1. Adventure Mode
   - Co-op survival
   - Explore maps, collect loot, avoid/fight monsters
   - Sell items for Company Coins
   - Use Stash to store items

2. Arena Mode
   - 6v6 PvP
   - Inspired by Bed Wars
   - Defend your ore, destroy the enemy team's ore
   - Guns: Revolver, Shotgun, Pickaxe
   - Infinite ammo

3. Broke AF Mode
   - Hardcore version of Adventure Mode

=== IMPORTANT SYSTEMS ===
- Tech Tree: Unlock skills and items using Research Points (RP)
- Company Coins (CC): Main currency
- Research Points (RP): Used in Tech Tree
- Stash: Permanent item storage
- Proximity Chat + Walkie-Talkies
- Physics toys and gadgets
- Weekly updates (usually every Tuesday)
